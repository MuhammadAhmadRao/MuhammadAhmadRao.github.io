### visual styling rules:
1. **near-grayscale base**: the interface container is quiet and light (`#f5f5f5` background, `#ffffff` window surface, `#111111` ink, `rgba(0,0,0,0.08)` borders).
2. **color through content**: color enters only through content (gifs, cards, stickers).
3. **soft rounded cards**: any asset with a solid/white background (`pixel-robot-hill.jpg`, `mascot-dino.jpg`) receives a soft rounded white card treatment (`border-radius: 12px; border: 1px solid rgba(0,0,0,0.08); box-shadow: 0 4px 14px rgba(0,0,0,0.06);`).
